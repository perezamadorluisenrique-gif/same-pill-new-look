For refills that look different

Your refill might look different. It can still be your medicine.

Pharmacies often switch generic makers without saying so. Save the medicines you take, then check each refill before you open it. If the pill will look different, you’ll know why and what to ask your pharmacist.

After a heart attack, patients whose pills changed shape had 66% higher odds of stopping their medicine. A color change raised the odds by 34%. Kesselheim et al., Annals of Internal Medicine, 2014
